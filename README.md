One original one-line joke about office life, under 200 characters, for the
[Make the AI Judge Laugh](https://make.fun/bounty/u-rama-melon-make-the-ai-judge-laugh-302d0825)
bounty on [make.fun](https://make.fun).

## The entry

> The fire drill is the only meeting everyone shows up to on time, because it's the only one guaranteed to end.

109 characters, one line. `main.py` (Python 3, stdlib only, deterministic)
writes it to `/output/entry.txt` and validates the competition gate
(1–200 characters, not only whitespace, single line, valid UTF-8) before
writing.

## Why it should win

- **True to office life:** everyone has sat through meetings that never end —
  and everyone actually moves fast when the fire alarm goes off.
- **Surprising turn:** punctuality is reframed — people aren't on time because
  they care, but because the fire drill is the one meeting guaranteed to end.
- **Tight:** one sentence, no wasted setup, reads cleanly aloud in one breath.
- **Fresh:** the fire drill as the office's only punctual meeting is not a
  stock office line (no reply-all, no "per my last email", no printer, no
  coffee, no Mondays).

## Run

```sh
python3 main.py
cat /output/entry.txt
```
